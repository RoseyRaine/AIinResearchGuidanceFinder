01 Research practiceComplementing, Not Replacing Academic Skills +

Scenario

Sarah, an HDR candidate studying ethnography, uses a GenAI tool to enhance her understanding of research methods. She asks the AI to generate an example of conducting field research in a hypothetical community to prepare for her fieldwork.

How GenAI helps

The AI tool provides a scenario of observing cultural practices in a community, helping Sarah visualise her approach. She also inputs an overview of her research topic and asks for possible chapter structures. These suggestions are not accepted blindly but are refined according to her critical assessment of existing literature.

Reflection

Sarah enhances her research preparation using AI as a complement, not a replacement. She remains responsible for critical thinking, creativity, and adapting AI insights to her unique research context.

02 Data & ethicsSafeguarding Data Privacy and Security +

Scenario

Alex is researching mental health interventions and wants to use GenAI to explore possible data analysis techniques. They recognise that uploading raw interview transcripts or other identifiable participant data into a public AI tool could breach privacy, ethics or data governance requirements.

How GenAI helps

Alex first uses synthetic or dummy data to test the analytical workflow and to understand how the method works. Before using any actual research data, Alex checks the project’s ethics approval, participant consent, data management plan and the conditions applying to the proposed AI tool. After confirming that the intended use is permitted, Alex uses an appropriate University-approved or protected environment and independently verifies the AI-assisted outputs.

Reflection

Alex distinguishes between using AI to learn or test an analytical approach and using AI to process actual research data. A University-approved tool may provide stronger privacy and security protections, but it does not automatically make all data or uses permissible.

03 Integrity & inclusionPractising Ethical Use and Integrity +

Scenario

Sophia, a PhD candidate in sociology, used GenAI tools to help refine her literature review, brainstorm new research directions, and identify gaps in existing studies. She realised that some sections of her thesis had been significantly influenced by the ideas generated through the AI tool.

How GenAI helps

Sophia used GenAI to brainstorm research questions by inputting a broad theme and generating specific topics. She also used the tool to refine her thesis language and to identify gaps in existing research. She included a clear acknowledgement statement and cited the tools she used.

Reflection

Sophia’s decision to acknowledge the AI’s influence, without presenting generated content as her own original work, exemplifies how researchers can ethically incorporate GenAI while maintaining integrity and compliance with research guidelines.

04 Literature & evidenceConducting Critical Evaluation of AI Outputs +

Scenario

Mark, an HDR candidate studying social media influence, uses GenAI to gather potential sources for his literature review. He notices that some references provided by the tool are fictitious.

How GenAI helps

Mark uses the AI tool to identify key areas of research but double-checks every reference and fact against academic databases like Google Scholar and Scopus. In one instance, the AI incorrectly cites a journal article, prompting him to find the correct source manually.

Reflection

Mark critically evaluates AI outputs, recognising that not all content is trustworthy. His attention to detail ensures his literature review is based on credible and verified sources.

05 Integrity & inclusionUpholding Inclusivity and Integrity +

Scenario

Emma, a researcher studying the portrayal of women in leadership roles, uses GenAI to draft a section of her thesis. Upon reviewing the AI-generated text, she observes that examples predominantly reference male CEOs, perpetuating a gendered stereotype of leadership.

How GenAI helps

The AI tool provides quick insights into prevailing discourses. However, upon noticing the imbalance, Emma challenges these gendered representations and seeks additional scholarly resources, including studies on female leadership in non-Western contexts, to broaden the scope of her analysis.

Reflection

Emma upholds inclusivity by actively identifying and correcting biased AI outputs. She ensures that her research amplifies underrepresented voices, addressing biases embedded in the AI’s training data.

06 Literature & evidenceLiterature Reviews +

Scenario

Priya, an honours student in public health, used a GenAI tool to get an overview of research on her topic. The tool produced a convincing-looking list of references, several of which turned out not to exist when she searched for them.

How GenAI helps

Priya used the AI-generated overview to identify key themes and terminology in her field. She then conducted her own systematic search in Scopus and PubMed using those terms, verifying each source independently before including it in her review.

Reflection

By using AI to orient herself in the field rather than to source her references, Priya avoided the risk of hallucinated citations while still benefiting from AI as a scoping tool.

07 Images & publishingAI-Generated Images +

Scenario

James, a PhD candidate in environmental science, used an AI image generator to produce a conceptual diagram of an ecosystem for inclusion in a conference presentation. He later considered whether the same image could be used in his journal article.

How GenAI helps

For the conference presentation, James disclosed the AI-generated origin of the diagram in his slide notes. For the journal article, he checked the journal’s author guidelines, which did not permit AI-generated figures. He recreated the diagram using a vector graphics tool based on his own research data.

Reflection

Context determines what is appropriate. Disclosure and publisher compliance are non-negotiable. Checking publisher-specific policies before submission saves significant revision work later.

08 Research practiceOngoing Learning and Adaptation +

Scenario

John is a communications researcher focusing on digital media trends. As AI tools continue to evolve, John stays updated on the latest advancements in NLP and attends AI-related workshops to integrate these advancements into his work.

How GenAI helps

John uses NLP-based AI tools to analyse sentiment across social media platforms. He frequently adapts his research methodology based on the latest features in AI tools, ensuring his work remains current. He also engages in academic discussions on AI ethics and attends AI-focused conferences to stay informed.

Reflection

John demonstrates a commitment to ongoing learning, continuously improving his research by incorporating the latest AI advancements. This adaptability ensures his research remains relevant in an evolving academic landscape.

09 Data & ethicsHuman Research Ethics +

Scenario

Lena, an HDR candidate in education, received ethics approval to conduct interviews with teachers about their classroom practices. After data collection, she considered using an AI tool to assist with thematic coding of the transcripts.

How GenAI helps

Lena reviewed her ethics application and noted that it did not specify AI-assisted analysis. She consulted her supervisor and sought an ethics amendment before proceeding. She confirmed that the proposed AI-assisted analysis, the data type and the selected tool were permitted. Only after receiving the necessary approval did she use an appropriate University-approved environment.

Reflection

Lena’s careful approach protected both her participants and the integrity of her research. Checking ethics obligations before introducing new analytical tools is essential.

10 Images & publishingPublishing +

Scenario

David, a postdoctoral researcher, used an AI tool to help him refine the language of a manuscript he was preparing for submission to an international journal. He had also used an AI image generator to produce a conceptual figure.

How GenAI helps

David checked the journal’s author guidelines, which required disclosure of AI-assisted language editing in the acknowledgements and prohibited AI-generated figures. He rewrote the figure using a graphics tool and added an appropriate acknowledgement of AI language assistance.

Reflection

Checking publisher-specific policies before submission, not after, saved David from having to revise his manuscript at a later stage.
