Transparency and declaration

Declaration examples

Adapt these examples to your thesis, honours, publisher and discipline requirements. Disclosure should be proportional to the nature and extent of AI involvement, and the requirements of the target journal or publisher should always be checked.

No generative AI used

No generative AI tools were used in the design, analysis, interpretation or writing of this thesis/research output.

Language editing only

Generative AI was used to improve clarity, grammar and expression in researcher-authored text. The intellectual content, analysis, interpretation and final wording remain my own. Logs of use are available on request.

Literature review support

Generative AI tools were used to assist with preliminary keyword generation and concept mapping for the literature review. All search strategies were finalised by the researcher, and all citations and claims were verified against bibliographic databases and original sources.

Data analysis support

Generative AI was used to assist with developing and checking analysis code and to suggest possible interpretations of de-identified/synthetic data. Final analysis decisions, verification and interpretation were undertaken by the researcher. Tool use, prompts, dates and verification steps are recorded in the project AI use log.

AI-assisted data analysis

[Tool/software and AI feature/model] was used to assist with [coding/classification/theme identification/statistical analysis/code development] of [describe the type of data without including confidential information]. Its use was consistent with the project’s ethics approval, participant consent and data management requirements where applicable. The researcher reviewed and verified all AI-assisted outputs and retained responsibility for analytical decisions and interpretation. Tool/model details, relevant prompts or settings, validation procedures and material limitations are documented in the research record.

Image or diagram generation

Figure X was generated using [tool name/version] on [date] from a prompt written by the author. The image was reviewed and edited for accuracy, accessibility and ethical appropriateness.

General substantive use

I acknowledge the use of [tool name and version] to [describe purpose, e.g. assist with preliminary concept mapping / refine language in researcher-authored sections / assist with developing analysis code]. I did not insert any output generated by these tools verbatim but used their output to inspire and develop my own ideas. Logs of use are available on request.

Use these as starting points, not fixed formulas. The guidance notes that researchers should adapt wording to local thesis, honours, publisher and discipline requirements. See Appendix C in the full guidance and Recommendation 3.
