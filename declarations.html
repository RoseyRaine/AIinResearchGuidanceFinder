Transparency and declaration

Declaration examples

Use the required Charles Sturt wording for HDR theses. For other research outputs, adapt these examples to the relevant publisher, funder, discipline and University requirements. Disclosure should be proportional to the nature and extent of AI involvement.

For Charles Sturt HDR theses

Use the current HDR Procedure wording

HDR candidates must follow the current Higher Degree by Research Procedure. Clause 96 specifies the AI assistance acknowledgement for a thesis where AI assistance has been used. Check the Procedure before submitting your thesis.

HDR candidates – AI assistance acknowledgement for theses

I acknowledge the use of [e.g. ChatGPT 4.0 (https://chat.openai.com/)] to complete my thesis and research. I used these tools to [e.g. identify limitations in past research, uncover potential measures, identify, but not correct, writing errors, conception and design of the project or output, analysis or interpretation of research data, drafting significant parts of the research output or critically revising it so as to contribute to its interpretation]. I did not insert any output generated by these tools verbatim - but instead used their output to inspire ideas.

Other research outputs

Adaptable examples

These examples may be adapted for Honours theses, manuscripts, reports, presentations and other research outputs. Always check the specific requirements of your target journal or publisher.

No generative AI used

No generative AI tools were used in the design, analysis, interpretation or writing of this thesis/research output.

Image or diagram generation

Figure X was generated using [tool name/version] on [date] from a prompt written by the author. The image was reviewed and edited for accuracy, accessibility and ethical appropriateness.

Literature Search and Brainstorming

Generative AI [AI tool & version] was used during the initial stages to help brainstorm search terms and structure conceptual outlines. No AI-generated text was directly copied into the final manuscript, and all cited literature was retrieved, read, and verified independently.

Language editing only

Generative AI was used to improve clarity, grammar and expression in researcher-authored text. The intellectual content, analysis, interpretation and final wording remain my own. Logs of use are available on request.

Literature review support

Generative AI tools were used to assist with preliminary keyword generation and concept mapping for the literature review. All search strategies were finalised by the researcher, and all citations and claims were verified against bibliographic databases and original sources.

Editorial and Proofreading Assistance Only

During the preparation of this work, the author(s) used [name AI tools e.g. Grammarly and ChatGPT-4o] for the purpose of copy-editing and improving the readability of prose. After using these tools, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the final publication.

Data analysis support

Generative AI was used to assist with developing and checking analysis code and to suggest possible interpretations of de-identified/synthetic data. Final analysis decisions, verification and interpretation were undertaken by the researcher. Tool use, prompts, dates and verification steps are recorded in the project AI use log.

General substantive use

I acknowledge the use of [tool name and version] to [describe purpose, e.g. assist with preliminary concept mapping / refine language in researcher-authored sections / assist with developing analysis code]. I did not insert any output generated by these tools verbatim but used their output to inspire and develop my own ideas. Logs of use are available on request.

AI-assisted data analysis

[Tool/software and AI feature/model] was used to assist with [coding/classification/theme identification/statistical analysis/code development] of [describe the type of data without including confidential information]. Its use was consistent with the project’s ethics approval, participant consent and data management requirements where applicable. The researcher reviewed and verified all AI-assisted outputs and retained responsibility for analytical decisions and interpretation. Tool/model details, relevant prompts or settings, validation procedures and material limitations are documented in the research record.

Language/editorial assistance for research outputs

I acknowledge the use of generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with the editing, rewriting, rephrasing and/or paraphrasing of parts of this thesis/dissertation to improve the clarity, readability and quality of the English language used. I declare that all intellectual content, analysis, interpretation, arguments, conclusions and scholarly contributions presented in this thesis/dissertation are my own. I take full responsibility for the accuracy, integrity and originality of the work submitted. The use of AI tools was limited to language and editorial assistance and did not replace my critical thinking, research activities, analysis or academic judgement. I confirm that all sources have been appropriately acknowledged and cited, and that my use of AI tools complies with the relevant policies, procedures and academic integrity requirements of Charles Sturt University. AI tool(s) used: [Insert name(s) of AI tool(s), version if known, and URL(s)]

After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the published article.

Record keeping

AI use log template

Keep a log where AI materially contributes to research design, methods, analysis, interpretation, writing, outputs or dissemination. The log does not need to capture every minor spelling or grammar check.

For defined research tasks such as screening, data extraction, coding, classification or risk of bias assessment, treat the prompt or prompt summary as part of the research method. Record material errors, discrepancies, instability or failure modes as well as successful use.
