Responsible and ethical use

Recommendations

The nine recommendations translate the core principles into practical research guidance. Open a recommendation for a concise overview, then follow the link to the full wording.

01RecommendationUse AI to Complement, Not Replace, Academic Skills+

Use AI to support thinking, learning and writing while retaining intellectual effort, methodological judgement and scholarly voice.

Read the full recommendation →View related case studies →
02RecommendationSafeguard Data Privacy and Security+

Classify what you plan to enter, understand the tool’s data handling, and do not assume that a paid, private or University-provided tool makes every research use permissible.

Read the full recommendation →
03RecommendationPractise Ethical Use and Integrity+

Record and disclose material AI use proportionately, retain responsibility for the work, and do not name AI as an author.

Read the full recommendation →See declaration examples →
04RecommendationConduct Critical Evaluation of AI Outputs+

Independently verify outputs, test for instability where AI performs a methodological function, and check for fabrication, bias, omissions and overconfidence.

Read the full recommendation →
05RecommendationUphold Inclusivity and Integrity+

Actively check outputs for narrow perspectives, stereotypes, exclusion, colonial assumptions and accessibility issues.

Read the full recommendation →
06RecommendationAI in Literature Reviews+

Use AI to support early scoping and bounded review tasks, but keep final searching, inclusion decisions, critical appraisal, interpretation and synthesis human-led.

Read the full recommendation →
07RecommendationTranslation and Multilingual Use+

Treat AI translations as drafts, protect confidential material, preserve meaning and disciplinary terminology, and use human review where appropriate.

Read the full recommendation →
08RecommendationAI-Generated Images and Multimodal Outputs+

Check accuracy, bias, copyright, ownership, publisher restrictions and disclosure requirements before using AI-generated visuals.

Read the full recommendation →
09RecommendationOngoing Learning and Adaptation+

Maintain contemporary AI literacy and use AI only when it is appropriate and proportionate to the task.

Read the full recommendation →
Need examples? The case studies show how these recommendations apply in practice. For wording you can adapt when reporting AI use, see Declaration examples.
